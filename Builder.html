    How this estimate works

    One target, all selected hits land, all actions initially ready. Timing is from the first action to the end of the last cast, including cooldown waits and attack windups. No travel time, movement, animation cancels, cooldown refunds or resource limits. With Target Settings enabled, each action uses the HP left by the previous action. Total damage includes overkill; HP stops at zero and later actions are hypothetical. Multi-hit and damage-over-time actions resolve together, without regeneration. Other combat conditions stay at their configured values.

    AA adds a non-critical attack; choose AA crit in Custom Action for a critical strike. Add bonus effects explicitly; they are not automatically triggered by the sequence. Ability rows can describe one component, a full duration, or bonus damage: choose the outcome you intend. Recast prerequisites and known windows are checked; other proc conditions are assumed satisfied.

    Missing damage and timing values count as 0 and show a red warning. Click an action to edit its values in seconds or damage after target defences; overrides remain fixed when your build changes. Hover or focus a damage number for its physical, magical and true damage breakdown.
